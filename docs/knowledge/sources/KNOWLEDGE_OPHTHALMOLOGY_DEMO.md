## Emergency and Urgency
Status: available

If this is a medical emergency, tell the caller to hang up and dial 911 or go to the nearest emergency room immediately.

New flashes or floaters require immediate transfer to office staff. Follow Human Transfer immediately for sudden vision loss or other eye emergencies.

To determine urgency when symptoms are unclear: Ask only for missing details, such as whether flashes or floaters are new, when symptoms started, or whether vision has changed. If flashes or floaters are unchanged and no eye emergency is described, continue standard scheduling.

## Scope of Services
Status: available

Clearbrook provides medical and surgical eye evaluations for cataracts, glaucoma, retina conditions, uveitis, pediatric ophthalmology, adult strabismus, double vision, and oculoplastic or eyelid concerns. Retina evaluations are available, while retina surgery is performed at an affiliated surgical facility rather than in the office.

Clearbrook also provides routine eye examinations, glasses prescriptions, contact-lens prescriptions and fittings, and optical services. Routine optometry is represented for patients age 7 and older. Children under 7 needing routine vision care use the medical scheduling lane for pediatric ophthalmology.

## Optical and Glasses
Status: available

Clearbrook's fictional optical department represents frames from YSL, Ferragamo, Gucci, Ray-Ban, Nike, Bebe, Anne Klein, Vivid, Lacoste, and Puma. Lens upgrades are available. Glasses usually take about 10 business days to arrive.

Eyeglass prescriptions are valid for 2 years, while a yearly eye exam is recommended. Patients may bring an outside prescription to shop for glasses. If they do not have a valid prescription, schedule a routine eye examination first.

Non-prescription sunglasses are represented in the demo and may be made with prescription lenses. Vision insurance generally does not cover non-prescription sunglasses.

Morgan Lee is the fictional licensed optician and is represented at Harbor Point Tuesday through Friday, 9:30 AM to 4:30 PM. No appointment is needed to browse frames, request adjustments, or ask about minor repairs.
