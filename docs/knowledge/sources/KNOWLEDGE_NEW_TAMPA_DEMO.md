## Emergency and Urgency
Status: available

For a true medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room immediately. Seek emergency care immediately instead of waiting for a physician text or callback. New flashes or floaters require immediate transfer to office staff. Sudden vision loss, a curtain or shadow over vision, eye trauma, chemical exposure, or severe eye pain with sudden vision symptoms also exit routine scheduling. To determine urgency, clarify only when needed; never delay an already urgent escalation for intake or insurance. If the caller says a three-month wait is too long for an urgent condition, transfer for staff assessment instead of treating the distant slot as clinically appropriate.

Status: available
