# Voice AI Observability

1. Establish the caller's intent from the available evidence.
2. Compare the agent's claims with tool results and final outcomes.
3. Treat sentiment as an evidence-backed signal and state uncertainty.
4. Classify each issue as confirmed, ambiguous, recovered, or healthy.
5. Cite the smallest sufficient evidence and explain caller impact.

Do not treat spoken claims, aggregate flags, or initiated actions as proof of a successful outcome.
