# Accepted Insurance Plans (Spring Hill)

## Common Aliases (check here first)
If the caller uses a shorthand name, this tells you what plan to use:
- "Simply" / "Simply Health" / "Simply Health Plans" → Simply Medicaid
- "Blue Cross" / "BCBS" → Florida Blue
- "BCBS Medicare HMO" → Florida Blue Medicare HMO
- "United" / "UHC" → United Healthcare
- "DuoComplete" → United Healthcare Dual Complete
- "Oscar" → Oscar Health
- "Humana" → ask which plan (Gold Plus, PPO, Medicare, etc.)
- "Molina" → ask: Medicaid, Medicare, or Marketplace?
- "Aetna EPO" → ask: North Broward or University of Miami?
- "Sunshine" → Sunshine Medicaid
- "Wellcare" → Wellcare
- "Staywell" → Staywell Medicare

## Not Accepted (Spring Hill)
Care Plus, Optimum Healthcare, Care Health Plus, Humana HMO (including Humana Premier HMO)
→ If a caller has one of these, let them know we don't accept that plan.
