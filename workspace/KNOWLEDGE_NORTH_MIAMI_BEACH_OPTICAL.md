## Optical / Glasses

North Miami Beach Optical stocks many major frame brands, including Gucci, Montblanc, YSL, Ray-Ban, Emporio Armani, and Cartier.

The office offers all types of lenses, including lens upgrades and treatments.

Eyeglass prescriptions are valid for up to 2 years, but a yearly eye exam is recommended to make sure the prescription is current and the patient has the right lenses to improve their vision.

If a patient wants glasses and already has a prescription, they can bring the prescription from their optometrist and choose from the office's frame selection. If they need a prescription, schedule them with the optometrist first, then they can browse frames and lenses.

Glasses are usually ready in less than 10 business days.

Frames include free adjustments and a two- or three-year warranty.

If a caller says their glasses are broken or asks whether they are under warranty, tell them to come in so the office can look at the frame or lenses.

## Contact Lenses

Contact lens prescriptions are valid for up to 1 year.

If a patient wants contact lenses and already has a prescription, they can bring the prescription from their optometrist. If they need a prescription, schedule them with the optometrist first.

If the patient's contact lens prescription is on file, the office can take a contact lens order over the phone with payment.

## Insurance

North Miami Beach Optical uses the same routine vision insurance source as Hollywood and Sweetwater optical.

For routine eye exams, glasses prescriptions, or contact lens prescriptions using accepted vision coverage, use `check_insurance` with coverageType `routine_vision`, then schedule with routing `optical_only`.

For every routine-vision patient, explain that some insurances do not cover retinal photos and there is a $39 charge. Collect the last 4 of the insured person's SSN for routine-vision insurance because some plans, including VSP, use it as the policy number.

Medical insurance checks are not supported for this office.
