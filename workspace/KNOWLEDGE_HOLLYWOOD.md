## Optical and Glasses
Status: available

Hollywood stocks many major frame brands, including Ray-Ban, Nike, Gucci, Chloe, Montblanc, Puma, Cartier, Lindberg, Mykita, and YSL.

Lens upgrades and treatments are available. The office offers all types of lenses, including upgrades and treatments.

Eyeglass prescriptions are valid for up to 2 years, but a yearly eye exam is recommended to make sure the prescription is current.

If a patient wants glasses and already has a prescription, they can bring the prescription from their optometrist and choose from the office's frame selection. If they need a prescription, schedule them with the optometrist first, then they can browse frames and lenses.

Glasses are usually ready in less than 10 business days.

Non-prescription sunglasses are available for purchase. If the patient wears prescription glasses, the office can customize prescription lenses into those frames. Most vision insurance plans do not cover non-prescription sunglasses. Sunglass brands include Maui Jim, Costa, Ray-Ban, Gucci, and many more.

Katie is the licensed optician. She has over 5 years of eyecare experience and specializes in frames, lenses, and fittings.

No appointment is needed to look at glasses or have current glasses adjusted. Sasha and Denise are in the office Monday–Thursday 8:30 AM–4:30 PM and Friday 8:30 AM–2:30 PM.

## Insurance and Referrals
Status: available

Hollywood has its own medical insurance list in the agent insurance rules. Use `check_insurance` with coverageType `medical` for medical or surgical visits.

Hollywood also supports routine vision. For routine eye exams, glasses prescriptions, or contact lens prescriptions using accepted vision coverage or self-pay, use `check_insurance` with coverageType `routine_vision`, then schedule with routing `optical_only`.

For every routine-vision patient, explain that some insurances do not cover retinal photos and there is a $39 charge.

Retina surgical care needs referral to a retina surgeon. If the caller does not have one, tell them to contact their primary care provider or insurance company for an appropriate referral.
