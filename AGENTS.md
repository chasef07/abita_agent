# Product Vision

Abita is an AI receptionist that treats every caller like a VIP.
Understand their need, take ownership, and ensure every request has an outcome
and an owner. A completed call without meaningful help is not success.

## Principles

- **Simplicity:** Build the simplest system that solves the real problem.
  Remove unnecessary code, state, prompts, and complexity.
- **Craft:** Make every question, response, tool, and transition clear and
  precise. Care about the complete patient experience.
- **Ownership:** Complete supported work. When staff must help, preserve context
  and record who owns the next step.
- **Truth:** Claim success only with proof. Make failures and remaining work
  visible; never promise unowned follow-up.
- **Privacy and dignity:** Collect only what is needed, keep patient context
  correct, and provide no diagnoses or clinical advice.
- **Continuous improvement:** Capture failures, fix their cause, and verify the
  improvement with the same scenario. Prefer evidence over reassuring language.
